Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Iskra Exchange LLC ("Iskra Exchange," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and retain information when you visit our website or contact us, including through our consultation form.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a consultation request, we may collect the information you choose to provide, including your full name, work email address, company name and website, job title, company size, the business challenges or goals you describe, and your preferred time slot and time zone.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information contained in your message and the contact details associated with it. Our website may also receive limited technical information that is ordinarily transmitted when a browser requests a webpage, such as device, browser, and server-log information. The information collected depends on the website hosting and technical services in use.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information submitted to us to respond to inquiries, prepare for and schedule consultations, understand a business's stated needs, communicate about potential services, operate and maintain the website, protect the website and our business, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate our website, receive and manage inquiries, communicate with you, or provide related business services. These providers may process information on our behalf for the services they provide. We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transaction, where permitted by law.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

Our consultation form is provided through Tally. When you submit the form, the information you enter is transmitted to and processed through Tally so that we can receive and manage your inquiry. Tally describes itself as a form-service provider and makes available a Privacy Notice and Data Processing Agreement. Tally's own handling of information through its services is also subject to its applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Read Tally's Privacy Notice and Tally's Data Processing Agreement.

5. Business Communications

We may use the contact information you provide to respond to your request, coordinate a consultation, follow up on a business inquiry, or communicate about services you have asked us to discuss. You may ask us to stop non-essential follow-up communications by contacting us.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps designed to protect information under our control. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain inquiry and contact information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and manage the inquiry, maintain relevant business records, provide requested services, resolve disputes, and meet legal or contractual obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the circumstances in which it was collected.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to websites or services operated by third parties. We do not control those third parties and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review the privacy notices of any third-party services you choose to use.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted, subject to applicable law and any records we are required or permitted to retain. We may need to verify your request before acting on it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date on this page. Changes take effect when posted unless otherwise stated.
