Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Iskra Exchange LLC ("Iskra Exchange," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and retain information when you visit our website or contact us, including through our consultation form.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a consultation request, we may collect the information you choose to provide, including your full name, work email address, company name and website, job title, company size, the business challenges or goals you describe, and your preferred time slot and time zone.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information contained in your message and the contact details associated with it.

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be transmitted automatically through your browser or processed by our website hosting and technology providers. This may include your IP address, browser type, device information, and server-log information. The information processed depends on the services and resources your browser accesses.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to business inquiries and consultation requests.
  • Schedule consultations and prepare for discussions.
  • Understand your business needs, challenges, and goals.
  • Communicate about potential services, proposals, and business engagements.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website and related technology.
  • Protect our website, systems, and business operations.
  • Meet applicable legal, contractual, and regulatory obligations.

3. How We Share Information

Iskra Exchange LLC does not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers that help us host and maintain our website, process consultation inquiries, manage communications, and support our business operations. These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to perform their services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, in response to valid legal process, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a business sale, merger, restructuring, or similar transaction, where permitted by law.

We do not authorize our service providers to use personal information submitted to Iskra for their own unrelated marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

Iskra Exchange LLC uses third-party services to host, maintain, and support its website and consultation process. These services may process limited technical information or other information as necessary to provide their respective services.

Website Hosting and Deployment: Our website is hosted through Vercel. We also use GitHub to store and manage our website's source code. Our GitHub repository is connected to Vercel to support website deployments and updates. In connection with these services, technical, account, repository, or deployment-related information may be processed.

Consultation Form: Our consultation form is provided through Tally. When you submit the form, the information you enter is transmitted to and processed through Tally so that we can receive and manage your inquiry.

Website Technology and Fonts: Our website uses Google Fonts to deliver certain typefaces and jsDelivr to deliver the Three.js library used for website visuals. When your browser requests these resources, the respective providers may receive technical information associated with that request, such as your IP address and browser information.

These providers operate under their own privacy notices and applicable service terms. Iskra Exchange LLC does not control their independent privacy practices.

You can review their privacy documentation here:

  • Vercel Privacy Notice
  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Tally Privacy Notice

5. Business Communications

We may use the contact information you provide to respond to your inquiries, coordinate consultations, follow up on business discussions, and communicate about services, proposals, or engagements you have asked us to consider.

You may request that we stop non-essential follow-up communications at any time by contacting us at main@iskraexchange.cc.

6. Data Security

Iskra Exchange LLC takes reasonable administrative and technical steps designed to protect personal information under our control from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

We also rely on third-party service providers to support certain website and business operations. Their security practices are governed by their respective policies and service agreements.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

7. Data Retention

Iskra Exchange LLC retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Uncompleted Consultation Inquiries: If an individual submits an inquiry but does not successfully schedule a consultation, we may retain their information for an initial period of 30 days to allow time to complete scheduling.

At the end of the 30-day period, we will send a reminder email notifying the individual that their inquiry has remained incomplete and that their personal information is scheduled for deletion. The individual will have an additional seven (7) days from the date of that notification to respond or complete scheduling.

If no response is received within those additional seven days, we will delete the inquiry information, subject to any legal obligations requiring retention.

Scheduled Consultations and Client Relationships: Information associated with successfully scheduled consultations, ongoing business discussions, client engagements, agreements, or business records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to manage those relationships, meet contractual obligations, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable laws.

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. If you follow an external link, you will be subject to that third party's website terms and privacy practices.

Iskra Exchange LLC does not control and is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their applicable privacy notices before providing personal information or using their services.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact Iskra Exchange LLC at main@iskraexchange.cc to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted.

We may ask you to verify your identity before processing a request. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Please note that certain information may need to be retained when required by law, for legitimate business records, or to resolve disputes and fulfill contractual obligations.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our website.

If we become aware that a child has provided personal information to us, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date on this page. Changes take effect when posted unless otherwise stated.
